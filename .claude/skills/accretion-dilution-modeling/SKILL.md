# Accretion / Dilution Modeling

Accretion is not value creation. Model it because the market reacts to it, then say so plainly in the same document.

## Techniques

1. **Model structure** — Standalone forecasts for both companies, a transaction assumptions block, a purchase price allocation, pro forma adjustments, and an EPS bridge. Every pro forma figure must equal a standalone figure plus a named adjustment.

2. **Purchase price allocation** — Equity price plus assumed debt less cash equals EV. Allocate to identified intangibles by class with useful lives, write up inventory and PP&E, book the deferred tax liability on the write-ups, and plug goodwill. State whether goodwill is tax-deductible under this structure.

3. **Intangible amortisation drag** — Estimate intangibles by class using sector-typical allocation percentages, build the amortisation schedule, and show the after-tax EPS drag per year. This is usually the largest single dilutive item and the one most often omitted from a quick calculation.

4. **The EPS bridge** — Acquirer net income, plus target net income, plus after-tax synergies, less after-tax incremental interest, less foregone interest on cash used, less after-tax intangible amortisation, less other adjustments, over pro forma diluted shares. Show every line in dollars and in cents per share.
   *Fails when:* synergies are added pre-tax while interest is deducted post-tax.

5. **Pro forma share count** — Acquirer shares, plus shares issued at the exchange ratio, plus dilution from converted target awards under the treasury method, less any concurrent buyback. Awards converting into acquirer stock are routinely missed.

6. **One-time versus recurring separation** — Inventory step-up, deferred revenue haircut, transaction fees expensed at close and integration costs are one-time; amortisation and interest are recurring. Present year-1 EPS with and without the one-time items and say which you consider the honest headline.

7. **Consideration mix comparison** — Compare all-cash, all-stock and mixed on accretion, pro forma leverage, rating impact, ownership dilution and whether a shareholder vote is triggered. The recommendation states what would change it.

8. **Break-even synergies** — Solve for the annual synergy required for year-1 and year-2 neutrality and compare it to the announced target. If break-even exceeds the target, the deal is dilutive on the company's own numbers.

9. **Break-even price** — Solve for the maximum price that keeps the deal accretive in the chosen year. Present it next to the DCF-based value ceiling; where the EPS constraint binds first, that is a fact the board needs stated.

10. **Contribution analysis** — Each party's share of pro forma revenue, EBITDA, net income and cash flow against its share of pro forma equity. This is the test of relative fairness in a stock merger and the basis of most public challenges to an exchange ratio.

11. **Exchange ratio mechanics** — Model fixed-ratio and fixed-value structures with any collar, showing consideration value and accretion as the acquirer's price moves ±20%. Identify the price at which the structure stops working.

12. **Rating headroom test** — Test pro forma metrics against the agency thresholds for the current rating and report the headroom in dollars of EBITDA. A downgrade changes the cost of the funding assumed in the model.

13. **ROIC and value test** — Compute deal ROIC against the acquirer's WACC by year, and value created as the present value of synergies less premium less integration cost. Where accretion and value disagree, explain which the board should weight.

14. **Model audit** — Sweep for double-counted synergies, interest on the wrong balance, tax applied twice, missing target awards in the share count, amortisation on non-amortising intangibles, and stub-period mismatches at a mid-year close.

## Quality bar

- [ ] Fiscal years aligned and the stub adjustment shown
- [ ] All synergy and interest items on a consistent tax basis
- [ ] Pro forma share count reconciles from acquirer standalone
- [ ] GAAP and cash EPS both presented
- [ ] Accretion shown next to the value conclusion, not instead of it

## Outputs

Merger model, PPA, EPS bridge, 3-year accretion summary, break-even analysis, consideration comparison, sensitivity grid, analyst Q&A.
