# Buyer Universe Mapping

The buyer list determines the price. A wider list raises tension; a wrong list leaks information and wastes the seller's one moment of leverage.

> **India:** load `india-transaction-regime` before applying this skill to an Indian company — Press Note 3 screens on beneficial ownership and the open-offer obligation changes what each buyer can afford.

## Techniques

1. **Segmented construction** — Strategic: direct competitors, adjacent players, vertical integrators, new entrants, international entrants. Financial: sector-focused sponsors, generalists, growth equity, family offices, long-hold capital, sponsor-owned platforms. Build each segment methodically before ranking any of them.

2. **Capacity filter** — Balance-sheet cash, leverage headroom, market cap relative to deal size, dry powder against remaining fund life, and whether the deal triggers a shareholder vote. Anyone who cannot pay is not a buyer, however good the fit.

3. **Willingness-to-pay from synergies** — Estimate each strategic buyer's specific synergy set and derive their ability to pay from it. This produces a ranked list built on economics rather than on who is well known.
   *Fails when:* buyers are ranked by size or reputation rather than by what the asset is worth to them specifically.

4. **Urgency assessment** — A publicly acknowledged capability gap, competitive pressure, a stated M&A ambition, an activist on the register, or a CEO in their first two years. Urgency moves price more reliably than strategic fit does.

5. **Process-behaviour profiling** — For each Tier 1 buyer, profile past process conduct: early or late bidder, bumps or holds, leads with structure, demands exclusivity, re-trades after diligence. Cite the deal that evidences each trait.

6. **Antitrust pre-screen** — Combined share in each overlapping product and geographic market, likely review path, remedy exposure and timeline. Rank buyers by regulatory risk separately from price, because the seller trades one against the other.

7. **FDI and national-security screen** — For cross-border buyers, assess the screening regime, precedent outcomes in this sector, and the realistic timeline. Some buyers are stalking horses rather than closers — know which.

8. **Confidentiality risk grading** — Grade each buyer by the competitive damage from what they would see and their leak history. This determines who enters the data room and when, not whether they are contacted.

9. **Tiering with contact waves** — Tier 1 contacted first, Tier 2 in wave two, Tier 3 only if the process underperforms, and a documented Do Not Contact list with reasons. Design the wave timing and the trigger for moving between waves.

10. **Process-type recommendation** — Broad auction, targeted process or bilateral, chosen from the number of credible buyers, confidentiality sensitivity and the seller's price-versus-speed preference. State the trade-off explicitly rather than defaulting to an auction.

11. **Tension design** — Determine the minimum number of credible second-round bidders needed to sustain price tension and define the specific action if fewer materialise. Tension is engineered, not hoped for.

12. **Relationship pathing** — Map existing relationships between the seller's board and management and each buyer's decision-makers. Recommend the warmest credible route to the first conversation — cold outreach converts at a fraction of the rate.

13. **Bid evaluation on certainty-adjusted value** — Rank bids on net expected proceeds after adjusting for structure, conditionality and closing probability, never on headline price.

## Quality bar

- [ ] Every buyer has a stated rationale and an estimated capacity
- [ ] Willingness-to-pay derived from buyer-specific synergies
- [ ] Antitrust and FDI screened before contact, not after
- [ ] Do Not Contact list documented with reasons
- [ ] Process recommendation justified by the universe, not by habit

## Outputs

Tiered buyer list with rationale, capacity and willingness-to-pay estimates, regulatory risk ranking, outreach wave plan, process recommendation, tracker design.
