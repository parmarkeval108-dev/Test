# Term Sheet Analysis

A term sheet is an economic document in legal clothing. Read it for who bears which risk and what each party can do later that they are not saying now.

## Techniques

1. **Economics extraction** — Convert every term into its economic effect in dollars: headline price, adjustments, escrow, earn-out at expected value, deferred consideration discounted, fees, and the certainty discount. Produce the net expected proceeds — that is the number being compared, not the headline.
   *Fails when:* bids are ranked on headline price when one carries a 20% escrow and a two-year earn-out.

2. **Bid comparison grid** — Compare every bid on the same axes: headline value, net expected proceeds, consideration form, conditionality, financing certainty, regulatory risk, timing, employee and site commitments, and deal-protection asks. Rank on certainty-adjusted value.

3. **Embedded option hunt** — Find every optionality the counterparty has been granted: walk rights, MAC triggers, financing outs, diligence conditions, price-adjustment discretion, and exclusivity without a standstill on their own process. Each is an option they hold and you wrote for free.

4. **Conditionality scoring** — Score each condition on the probability of non-satisfaction and the time it adds. Convert the total into a probability of closing and a discount to the headline.

5. **Exclusivity terms** — Length, extension triggers, break rights, what the seller may still do, and what happens on expiry. Exclusivity is the moment a seller's leverage peaks and then falls — the terms decide how fast.

6. **Deal protection package** — Matching rights, no-shop with fiduciary out, break fee, reverse break fee, force-the-vote. Benchmark each against precedent for this deal size and type, and state which the board's fiduciary duties permit.

7. **Credit term extraction** — For financing term sheets: leverage, pricing grid, floors, OID, call protection, amortisation, sweep step-downs, EBITDA definition and add-back caps, covenant levels and headroom, incremental facility capacity, restricted payment and asset sale baskets, MFN protection and its sunset, portability. The baskets are where flexibility is actually granted.

8. **EBITDA definition scrutiny** — In a credit term sheet, the add-back definition is worth more than 25bps of spread. Read the cap, the look-forward period, and whether synergies require third-party verification.

9. **Market benchmarking** — Compare every material term against current market for this credit or this deal type, using recent comparable transactions rather than a general sense of market. Mark each term as aggressive, market, or off-market.

10. **Ask-and-concede mapping** — Before negotiating, categorise every term as must-have, tradeable or give-away, and identify which of the counterparty's asks are cheap for you to grant. Negotiation is trade, and the trade list is prepared in advance.

11. **Definitive-agreement forward look** — Identify the terms deliberately left vague that will be fought over in the SPA: adjustment mechanics, warranty scope and survival, indemnity caps and baskets, materiality scrapes, and the disclosure standard. Nail them at term-sheet stage or expect to lose them later.

12. **Re-trade vulnerability assessment** — Identify where the term sheet gives the buyer grounds to reopen price after diligence, and close those routes. Precise definitions and a short exclusivity are the defence.

13. **Non-binding-with-teeth drafting** — Keep the commercial terms non-binding while making exclusivity, confidentiality, costs and governing law binding. Say so explicitly in the document.

## Quality bar

- [ ] Every term converted to its economic effect
- [ ] All bids compared on net expected proceeds, not headline
- [ ] Every counterparty option identified and priced
- [ ] Every material term benchmarked against named precedent
- [ ] Ask-and-concede list prepared before negotiation starts

## Outputs

Term extraction table, bid comparison grid, market benchmark, optionality register, negotiation position, recommendation memo.
