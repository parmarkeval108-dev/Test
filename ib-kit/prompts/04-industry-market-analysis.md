# Industry & Market Analysis Prompts

**Layer:** Insight · **Engagement contexts:** All
**Pairs with:** `skills/industry-analysis`, `agents/market-intelligence-agent`, `workflows/pitchbook.md`

## A. Market sizing and structure

1. **Size the market** — "Size the `<market>` for `<year>`. Build it top-down and bottom-up independently, reconcile the two, and state which you trust and why. Define the boundary of what you are counting before you count it."
2. **Segment the market** — "Segment `<market>` by product, customer type, channel and geography. Give size, growth and margin per segment and identify where profit actually pools."
3. **TAM / SAM / SOM** — "Separate total addressable, serviceable addressable and serviceable obtainable market for `<Company>`. Be explicit about the constraint that reduces each step."
4. **Growth decomposition** — "Decompose `<market>` growth into volume, price, mix and substitution. Identify which component is durable."
5. **Share map** — "Build a market share table for `<market>`: top 10 players, share, 3-year share change, and the strategic move that caused each shift."
6. **Concentration metrics** — "Compute CR4, CR8 and HHI for `<market>`. Interpret them against the relevant competition-authority thresholds."
7. **Fragmentation opportunity** — "Quantify the long tail of `<market>`: number of operators below `<$X>`m revenue, aggregate share, and consolidation precedent."
8. **Value-chain margin pools** — "Map the `<market>` value chain and allocate industry profit across stages. Identify who captures value and why."
9. **Cost-curve analysis** — "Build the industry cost curve for `<market>`. Place `<Company>` on it and state its position's durability."
10. **Capacity and utilisation** — "Assess `<market>` capacity, utilisation and announced additions. Identify whether the market is heading into over- or under-supply and when."

## B. Competitive dynamics

11. **Five forces, quantified** — "Run a five-forces analysis on `<market>` where every force is backed by a number, not an adjective. No unsupported assertions."
12. **Competitor profiles** — "Profile the top `<N>` competitors: strategy, financials, capacity, recent investments, M&A history, and their most likely next move."
13. **Competitive response modelling** — "If `<Company>` cuts price by `<X>`%, model the likely response of each major competitor and the resulting industry margin outcome."
14. **Barriers to entry** — "Assess entry barriers in `<market>`: capital intensity, regulation, distribution, switching costs, network effects, IP, scale. Name the most recent successful entrant and how they got in."
15. **Switching-cost analysis** — "Quantify customer switching costs in `<market>` in money and time. Test it against observed churn rates."
16. **Disruption scan** — "Identify the three most credible disruption vectors for `<market>` — technology, business model, regulation — with evidence of early traction, not speculation."
17. **Substitute threat** — "Map substitutes for `<market>`'s core product, their price-performance trajectory, and the crossover point where substitution accelerates."
18. **Supplier power** — "Assess supplier concentration and input-cost pass-through in `<market>`. Quantify the margin impact of a `<X>`% input-cost move."
19. **Customer power** — "Assess buyer concentration and procurement sophistication. Quantify historical price erosion per annum."
20. **Consolidation history** — "Chart 10 years of M&A in `<market>`: volume, value, multiples, acquirer type. Identify what the current consolidation phase is."

## C. Demand, cycle and macro

21. **Demand drivers** — "Identify the top 5 demand drivers for `<market>` and quantify the elasticity of demand to each. Cite the evidence."
22. **Cycle positioning** — "Place `<market>` on its cycle. Define the cycle's length and amplitude historically, state where we are, and name the indicator that would confirm a turn."
23. **Leading indicators** — "Define a leading-indicator dashboard for `<market>`: 6–8 series, their lead time, their source and their update frequency."
24. **Macro sensitivity** — "Quantify `<market>`'s sensitivity to GDP, rates, FX, energy prices and `<sector-specific variable>`. Express each as a beta with a historical basis."
25. **Recession stress case** — "Model `<market>` through a `<X>`% GDP contraction using the `<year>` downturn as the template. State volume, price and margin outcomes."
26. **Inflation pass-through** — "Assess historical pass-through of input inflation to price in `<market>`. Give the lag in months and the leakage percentage."
27. **Scenario set** — "Build three `<year>`–`<year>` scenarios for `<market>` — base, upside, downside — each with named drivers, quantified outcomes and the observable signposts that would confirm it."
28. **Long-term structural view** — "Set out the 10-year structural view for `<market>`: what changes, what does not, and which of today's players survive the change."

## D. Regulation, technology and ESG

29. **Regulatory map** — "Map the regulatory regime governing `<market>` by jurisdiction: regulator, key rules, pending changes, enforcement posture."
30. **Pending regulation impact** — "Assess the effect of `<regulation>` on `<market>` economics: compliance cost, capex requirement, competitive redistribution, and timing."
31. **Licensing and permitting** — "Describe licensing requirements in `<market>`, typical time to obtain, and whether licences transfer on a change of control."
32. **Technology roadmap** — "Set out the technology roadmap for `<market>` over 5 years: what is in production, in pilot, and in research, with the incumbent's exposure to each."
33. **ESG materiality** — "Identify the financially material ESG factors for `<market>` using a recognised materiality framework. Quantify the cost or revenue at stake for each."
34. **Decarbonisation pathway** — "Model the capex and operating-cost implications of `<market>`'s decarbonisation trajectory to `<year>`."
35. **Supply-chain resilience** — "Map `<market>`'s supply chain concentration by geography and tier. Identify single points of failure and quantify the disruption cost."
36. **Trade and tariff exposure** — "Assess `<market>`'s exposure to tariffs, export controls and trade restrictions. Quantify the margin effect of `<scenario>`."

## E. Synthesis and delivery

37. **Industry primer** — "Write a 5-page industry primer on `<market>` for a client team new to the sector: how it works, who the players are, what drives economics, what to worry about."
38. **Investment thesis** — "State the bull case, the bear case and the base case for `<market>` in 200 words each, with the three facts that decide between them."
39. **Sector-page for a pitch** — "Build the market-overview page for `<Client>`'s pitchbook: one market-size chart, one share chart, one driver chart, and three takeaway bullets that a banker can deliver from memory."
40. **Source quality audit** — "List every source used in this analysis with its publisher, date, methodology and known bias. Flag anything where a vendor's estimate is being treated as fact."
41. **What we do not know** — "State the five most important things we do not know about `<market>`, why they are unknown, and what expert call or dataset would resolve each."
42. **Read-across to the target** — "Translate this industry view into specific implications for `<Company>`'s forecast: which line items move, by how much, and in which year."
