# Management Questions & Diligence Prompts

**Layer:** Insight · **Engagement contexts:** M&A Advisory, Leveraged Finance, Restructuring
**Pairs with:** `skills/diligence-questioning`, `agents/diligence-analysis-agent`, `workflows/diligence.md`

## A. Question design

1. **Build the question list** — "Build the management question list for `<Company>` organised by function. Each question must be answerable, specific, and aimed at a fact we cannot get from documents. Mark each as diagnostic, confirmatory or deal-breaking."
2. **Prioritise ruthlessly** — "We have 90 minutes with the CEO and CFO. Select the 12 questions that matter most and order them so that each answer informs the next. Explain the sequencing logic."
3. **Question quality audit** — "Review this question list and delete every question that is answerable from the CIM, leading, compound, or too vague to produce a usable answer. Show what you removed and why."
4. **Follow-up trees** — "For each of the top 10 questions, write the follow-up for the three most likely answers: the good answer, the evasive answer and the bad answer."
5. **Disconfirming questions** — "Write the questions designed to disprove our investment thesis rather than confirm it. These are the ones we most want to ask and are most likely to skip."
6. **Question the omissions** — "Read the CIM and identify what is conspicuously absent: a missing metric, a period skipped, a segment merged, a customer not named. Turn each omission into a question."

## B. Commercial diligence questions

7. **Revenue quality** — "Write the questions that test revenue quality: recognition policy, contract terms, the split between recurring and one-off, multi-year commitments, and any revenue with a service obligation attached."
8. **Customer economics** — "Write the questions on customer economics: acquisition cost, payback period, lifetime value, gross and net retention, cohort behaviour, and the definition used for each."
9. **Top customer deep-dive** — "For the top 10 customers, write the questions on contract term, renewal date, pricing mechanism, volume commitment, termination and change-of-control rights, and the relationship owner."
10. **Pricing power** — "Write the questions that test pricing power: last price increase, realisation versus list, customer pushback, competitive tendering frequency, and contractual price escalators."
11. **Pipeline and backlog** — "Write the questions on pipeline definition, stage conversion history, backlog composition, cancellation rights and the difference between contracted and expected revenue."
12. **Competitive position** — "Write the questions that reveal true competitive position: win/loss rates, the competitor most often faced, deals lost in the last year and why, and what customers say at renewal."
13. **Churn forensics** — "Write the questions that get behind the churn number: the definition used, gross versus net, whether downgrades count, seat-level versus logo-level, and the reason coding."

## C. Financial diligence questions

14. **Quality of earnings** — "Write the questions that test each EBITDA add-back: what the cost was, whether it recurs, what evidence supports removing it, and whether the related activity continues."
15. **Working capital** — "Write the questions on working capital: monthly seasonality, the normalised level, any period-end management of receivables or payables, and factoring or supply-chain finance arrangements."
16. **Capex discipline** — "Write the questions separating maintenance from growth capex: the definition used, deferred maintenance, asset age and condition, and the capex required in the next three years regardless of growth."
17. **Cash conversion** — "Write the questions explaining the gap between EBITDA and operating cash flow over three years, line by line."
18. **Budget accuracy** — "Write the questions on forecasting discipline: budget versus actual for three years, who sets the targets, what is in the plan that has never been done before, and the plan's dependency on named deals."
19. **Accounting policy** — "Write the questions on judgemental policies: revenue recognition, capitalisation, provisions, inventory valuation, impairment testing, and any policy change in the last three years."
20. **Related parties** — "Write the questions on related-party transactions: counterparties, terms, whether they are arm's length, and what happens to each on a change of control."
21. **Off-balance-sheet** — "Write the questions on off-balance-sheet exposure: guarantees, contingent liabilities, operating commitments, JV obligations, and letters of credit."
22. **Tax exposure** — "Write the questions on tax: open audits, uncertain positions, transfer pricing documentation, historic restructurings, and any position that would not survive review."

## D. Operational, legal and people questions

23. **Operations and capacity** — "Write the questions on capacity, utilisation, bottlenecks, yield, downtime and the investment required to support the growth plan."
24. **Supply chain** — "Write the questions on supplier concentration, single-source components, contract terms, lead times, and the plan if the largest supplier fails."
25. **Technology and IT** — "Write the questions on the technology estate: core systems, age, technical debt, cloud position, planned migrations, and the cost of the next three years of IT investment."
26. **Cybersecurity** — "Write the questions on security posture: incidents in the last three years, assessment results, insurance coverage, and remediation commitments outstanding."
27. **Intellectual property** — "Write the questions on IP ownership, registration, contributor assignments, open-source usage and licence compliance, and any current or threatened dispute."
28. **Legal and litigation** — "Write the questions on litigation: open matters, quantum, insurance, provisions taken, and any pattern that suggests a systemic issue."
29. **Regulatory compliance** — "Write the questions on licences, inspections, findings, remediation status, and anything that does not transfer on a change of control."
30. **ESG and environmental** — "Write the questions on environmental liabilities, site contamination, remediation obligations, emissions commitments and supply-chain labour standards."
31. **Organisation and talent** — "Write the questions on the organisation: structure, span of control, attrition by level, open critical roles, succession, and the five people whose departure would matter most."
32. **Compensation and retention** — "Write the questions on compensation: structure, benchmarking, equity outstanding, change-of-control triggers, and existing retention commitments."
33. **Culture and management** — "Write the questions that reveal how decisions actually get made, what the last hard decision was, and what management disagrees about."
34. **Pension and benefits** — "Write the questions on pension schemes: funding status, valuation basis, contribution schedule, trustee relationship, and any regulatory clearance needed on a change of control."

## E. Conducting and using the session

35. **Session agenda** — "Design the management session: agenda, timing, who attends from each side, what is presented versus discussed, and how time is protected for the hard questions."
36. **Interviewer brief** — "Brief the team: who asks what, who takes notes, how to handle an evasive answer, and the signal for moving on."
37. **Answer evaluation** — "Evaluate these management answers `<answers>`. For each: is the question answered, is it supported by evidence, is it consistent with the documents, and what is the follow-up?"
38. **Evasion detection** — "Review this transcript and identify every question that was not actually answered, every answer that changed a definition mid-sentence, and every place where a specific question got a general answer."
39. **Consistency cross-check** — "Cross-check management's answers against the CIM, the financial statements and prior-period statements. List every inconsistency with the specific figures."
40. **Findings memo** — "Write the diligence findings memo: what we confirmed, what we disproved, what remains open, the value impact of each finding, and the three findings that change the price or the structure."
41. **Second-session list** — "From the first session's answers, build the follow-up question list for the second session, ordered by how much each answer affects value."
42. **Expert-call brief** — "Design the expert-call brief on `<topic>`: the profile of expert we need, the questions to ask, the questions we must not ask for confidentiality reasons, and how we will validate what they tell us."
